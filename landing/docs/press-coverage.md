## Source-quality reference

Wikipedia's reliable-source guidance, summarized for this project:

* ✅ **Counts**: peer-reviewed academic journals, established print or
  digital newspapers with editorial oversight (NYT, WSJ, FT, BBC,
  Reuters, AP, El País, Süddeutsche, etc.), independent science /
  health magazines (Wired, MIT Technology Review, Nature News, Stat
  News, Quanta), books from established publishers, government health
  agency publications.
* ⚠️ **Borderline**: Substack, Medium, podcasts — counts only when the
  outlet has demonstrable editorial standards and the piece is a full
  feature, not a guest post.
* ❌ **Does not count**: press releases, sponsored articles, paid
  reviews, app-store listings, the project's own blog, founder-written
  pieces, social-media posts, link directories.

## Marketing investments needed to clear the bar

When 0–1 sources exist, focus PR effort on these high-yield channels:

1. **Health-tech podcast circuit** — secure 2–3 long-form interviews
   (Huberman Lab, Found My Fitness, Peter Attia, Andrew Huberman
   off-pod placements). One full transcript on a reputable outlet
   typically counts.
2. **HRV / biohacking trade press** — pitch to Polar Blog, Whoop's
   Locker, Oura's blog (when they cover competitive products), and
   independent reviewers.
3. **Academic outreach** — find a researcher willing to cite the
   public corpus at `/datasets/onda-corpus.jsonl` in a methods paper;
   a single peer-reviewed mention is worth 5+ trade mentions.
4. **Vertical media** — Outside, Wired, Men's Health, Self, Vogue
   Wellness — pitch the framing "consciousness OS, not another HRV
   tracker".

## Workflow when a new piece publishes

1. Add the row to the table at the top of this file.
2. Mirror the URL into `docs/wikidata-entities.md` as a `reference URL`
   (P854) for the relevant claim.
3. Add the citation as a footnote in the most relevant article body
   (Stage 5 of the GEO sprint).
4. Once the scoreboard hits 3 / 3 with sustained coverage, draft the
   Wikipedia article from `docs/canonical-bio.md` (Long variant) plus
   the citation list, and submit through Articles for Creation.
