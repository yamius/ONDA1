# 12. Translation terms (science pages and their translations)

Binding terminology for `content/science-i18n/<lang>/` and for any translated article or glossary text that repeats these terms. Owner decisions; change only with Yakiv's approval.

## HRV

| Language | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| all | Write **HRV**; give the full name at the first prose mention | ru «вариабельность сердечного ритма (HRV)» |
| es, fr, pt | Add the local abbreviation at the first mention | es «(HRV o VFC)», fr «(HRV, ou VFC)», pt «(HRV ou VFC)» |
| ru, uk | HRV is **masculine** (spoken usage of watch users) | «низкий HRV», «ваш HRV» |
| pl | HRV is **neuter** (owner decision 2026-10-06; the majority form on the site and in spoken Polish) | «niskie HRV», «niskoczęstotliwościowe HRV» — never «niska HRV» |
